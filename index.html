Don't forget your password!
If you ever forget your password, you can always reset the data and history on your browser and re-launch the setup, or click "forgot password" on login. However, if you reset your browser data and history you will lose other stored data, so it's best to reset the password without risking any data (e.g. logging into other unrelated Material OS sites). This is your last chance to go back and change your password to something else if needed.
